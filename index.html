Quillset Labs userscripts

Small browser userscripts for Tampermonkey and Violentmonkey.

  • Quillset Reader: a free reader mode: turns the article you are reading into a clean, single-column view, with font size, line width and light or dark themes. Runs only in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Quillset Reader Premium: the paid build: extra themes, per-site settings and a print-friendly layout.

Install links and the changelog will appear here with the first release.
